Meet our musicians!

Special Guest Performers

Sri Lalit Subramanian

Sri Lalit Subramanian is an accomplished Indian Classical vocalist and percussionist from Cleveland, OH. Originally from Pune, India, Sri Lalit received his vocal training studying Carnatic music under Sri Tiruvarur Girish, Sri Neyveli Santhanagopalan, Smt. Alamela Mani, and Hindustani music under Pandit Shekhar Kumbhojkar. His percussion training includes Tabla under Pandit Anand Godse and Mridangam under his cousin Sri Vijay Shriram, who is the nephew and disciple of Guru Karaikkudi Mani. A Sangeet Visharad and a YuvaWani artist with All India Radio, Sri Lalit has performed at major venues including the Kennedy Center, Krannert Center, Walt Disney Concert Hall, Lincoln Jazz Center, Cleveland Thyagaraja Aradhana, and Sabhas in India. Sri Lalit also performs Mridangam and vocals with the Cleveland-based fusion Jazz band ‘Infusion.’ and has also sung playback for a Marathi film. Beyond performance, Sri Lalit is a dedicated educator, running his music school Madhuralalya, serving as a guest lecturer on World Music at the Cleveland Institute of Music, and also freelancing as a guest music teacher for K–12 schools in the Cleveland suburbs. He has also led master classes at various institutions such as Case Western Reserve University, University of Michigan, Florida State University, University of Illinois Urbana–Champaign. He maintains a full-time career as a principal engineer at a med tech firm, specializing in radiation oncology.

Keshav Coimbatore

Keshav Coimbatore is an Indian classical music student, a disciple of Sri Lalit Subramanian for Carnatic vocal music, tabla, and mridangam, and a disciple of Smt. Shruti Aring for violin. He began learning tabla at seven years old and developed a passion for accompanying bhajans on the tabla and harmonium.

Outside of music, Keshav is a high school sophomore actively involved in robotics and Model UN and is the vice president of Stock Market Club.
