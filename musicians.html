Our Musicians

The maestros bringing Ithihasa Anubhava to life

Featured Artists

Ithihasa Anubhava is accompanied by a gifted ensemble of classical musicians performing live throughout the evening. Their artistry provides the sonic foundation for each dance piece, weaving together centuries of classical Indian musical tradition.

Instruments

The performance features a rich palette of classical instruments including the mridangam, violin, veena, tabla, and nattuvangam, each lending its distinctive voice to the choreographies of Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Kuchipudi, and Odissi.

Vocal Music

Classical Carnatic and Hindustani vocal compositions accompany several pieces throughout the show, with singers performing live to complement the movements of the dancers on stage.
