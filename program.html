An evening of classical Indian dance & music

About the Show

Ithihasa Anubhava — meaning "the experience of history" — is an evening-length production celebrating the living traditions of classical Indian dance. NRITYA and NAADAM together present performances spanning Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Kuchipudi, and Odissi, each rooted in centuries of mythology, devotion, and storytelling.
